What this version does

This version generates a topic-based script, synthesizes narration locally, animates an original cartoon host and generated abstract game-style scenes, adds timed captions and exports a narrated vertical WebM. It does not copy Peter Griffin, use stolen gameplay footage, or generate photorealistic AI video clips. The visual scenes are original animated graphics. MP4 conversion may require a separate converter; WebM works in modern browsers and can be uploaded where supported.

No unlimited external AI video generation is possible for free. This version avoids paid video APIs by making the visuals itself.
